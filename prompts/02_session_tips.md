# Session Tips — Prompts to Use During the SDD Session

Use these during the session when the conversation needs a nudge.

---

## When the AI gets too agreeable

```
What's wrong with what I just said? Push back.
```

---

## When you want an opinion, not a list of options

```
Don't give me options. Tell me what you would recommend and why.
Then I'll tell you if I agree.
```

---

## When the AI makes up a number

```
What's the basis for that number? Did you calculate it or estimate it?
Show your work.
```

---

## When you think a section is complete

```
Do a complete pass on everything we've discussed so far.
Tell me what you've captured, verify it against our conversation,
and flag anything missing or still marked ❓ or 🔄.
```

---

## When you want to check if the spec is ready to build from

```
Could a developer who was not in this conversation pick up this spec 
and build exactly what I have in my head — without asking me anything?
Tell me honestly what's missing.
```

---

## When the AI jumps ahead

```
Slow down. We haven't locked [topic] yet. Come back to that.
```

---

## When you want to close the session

```
We're done for today. Summarize every ✅ locked decision, every 🔄 open 
debate, and every ❓ unknown. Then tell me what section to tackle next 
session to make progress toward a complete SDD.
```
